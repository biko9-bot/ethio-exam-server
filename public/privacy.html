{{APP_NAME}} helps Grade 6 and Grade 8 students in Ethiopia practise for their national ministry exams. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and what you can do about it.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your first name, last name, phone number, grade and region. Your password is stored only as a one-way (hashed) value; we cannot read it.
  • Payment proof: if you buy a subscription, the screenshot of your CBE or Telebirr receipt that you upload, plus the plan, amount and any transaction reference you type.
  • Study activity: your practice results (subject, exam year, score, time taken) so you can see your history and progress.
  • Technical logs: your IP address and the time of requests, kept briefly on our server for security and abuse prevention.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos (other than the receipt you choose to upload), microphone, or advertising identifiers. The app contains no advertising.

How we use it

  • To create your account and let you log in.
  • To check your payment and unlock the content you paid for.
  • To save your practice history and show your progress.
  • To protect the service from fraud and abuse and to contact you about your account or payment if needed.

Sharing

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers. It is stored on a server run by our hosting provider, who processes it only to host the service. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Children

This app is made for school students, including children. We collect only what is needed to run the service (listed above). If you are under the age of consent in your country, please use the app with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian may ask us to see or delete a child's information at any time using the contact details below.

Retention and deletion

We keep your account and study history while your account exists. You can delete your account and its data at any time inside the app (Profile → Delete account) or on this page: delete your account. When you delete your account we remove your profile, subscription record, payment receipts and practice history. Server security logs and backups may keep limited technical records for a short period before they are overwritten.

Security

Data is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Passwords are hashed, payment screenshots are visible only to the administrators who verify payments, and access to the server is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.
